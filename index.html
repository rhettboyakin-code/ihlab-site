The opportunity

Generosity is already here. Connection is not.

Utah’s funders, founders, and frontline leaders are ready to do more. Too often they work in parallel: capable, committed, and still isolated from each other. IHLab is the shared practice space that turns goodwill into coordinated impact.

Three moves, one arc:

  1. 01
    See people as investable.

    Move from pity to partnership.

  2. 02
    Lead with dignity under pressure.

    Use the Dignity Index when conflict gets expensive.

  3. 03
    Build organizations that last.

    Align money, people, and mission so care helps rather than harms.

A premium cohort experience

Eight sessions. Real takeaways. A network that stays.

IHLab convenes CEOs, executive directors, founders, and civic innovators for a four-month cohort at The Other Side Village. Each gathering pairs a focused talk with workshopping and table conversation led by Dignity Fellows. You leave with tools you can use Monday, and peers who know your work by name.
