Why this lab

Utah funds care generously. The people who run it rarely train together.

Funders, founders, and frontline operators across Utah already carry real budgets and real people. IHLab puts fifty of them in one Salt Lake City cohort with UAC, The Other Side Leadership Institute, and Dignity.Us so the next hire, grant, and hard conversation get sharper together.

Three moves:

  1. 01
    See people as worth investing in.

    Fund and manage as partners, not as cases.

  2. 02
    Lead with dignity when conflict costs money and trust.

    Use the Dignity Index in the room where it matters.

  3. 03
    Align money, people, and mission so care builds capacity inside your organization.

How we work

Generous listening. Clear accountability. Shared tools.

Dignity Index practice when conflict costs money and trust. Peer tables that learn your organization by name. Care that builds capacity inside the systems you already lead.

The earliest investment pays the greatest dividends.
